About me

Thoughtful websites, built with care.

I'm Iqra Rizwan, a WordPress developer who enjoys turning ideas into clean, fast and easy-to-manage websites. I work across the WordPress toolkit — building layouts with Elementor, setting up online stores with WooCommerce, and writing custom plugins when a site needs something off-the-shelf tools can't provide.

My projects include a restaurant website with its own AI-powered chatbot plugin, a WooCommerce fashion store and a clinic website with online appointment booking. I care about responsive layouts, small details and websites that owners can confidently update themselves.
